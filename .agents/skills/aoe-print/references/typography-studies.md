## T01 — Quiet text against a dominant image

Source locator: user screenshot 2026-10-05 at 8.58.16 PM.

**Observed:** A blurred dark profile occupies most of a pale portrait field. Near the lower left, a compact warm-colored cluster contains a radial mark, a two-line serif statement, and denser uppercase text with a monospaced appearance. The statement is larger than the diagnostic-looking text, but both are small relative to the silhouette. Their left edges align. Sparse small marks and lower-right diagonal stripes sit outside the cluster.

**Interpretation:** The silhouette establishes mood before the text supplies the subject. The serif statement and diagnostic register suggest a contrast between a person and a system reading that person. A visually small block gains attention from placement, isolation, and contrast; it does not need to become a large headline.

**Transfer:** Give the image the long first look; build a compact reading sequence within one text cluster. Differentiate the statement from evidence or metadata through type character, spacing, and moderate scale change. Preserve a calm field instead of enlarging the text to fill it.

**Tradeoff:** Fine warm text over a textured dark field may be difficult at distance. This screenshot does not prove print legibility. For a street event poster, keep essential identifying details readable at the required distance, even if that requires departing from this reference. Do not infer a required tiny-text style.

## T02 — Dense type and illustration as one field

Source locator: user screenshot 2026-10-05 at 8.58.27 PM.

**Observed:** Dark musicians and dancers surround large central festival lettering on a blue field. Tall serif display forms dominate the text area. The upper title line has varied letter scale/placement; the lower line forms a more continuous band. Date and venue appear above the main title toward opposite sides. Other short information blocks occupy side gaps and outlined ovals below. The same dark ink-like color ties text and figures together.

**Interpretation:** The title has a conspicuous silhouette and central position, while recurring serif forms and illustration unify a crowded field. Local shifts in size and position suggest rhythm. Secondary information is assertive compared with T01, yet the title remains substantially more prominent. Density contributes to the festival character.

**Transfer:** Fit words and image silhouettes together around a clear reading anchor. Let short information blocks occupy purposeful pockets. Establish repeating forms or alignments so density feels related rather than randomly packed. Preserve the recognizable word shapes when changing individual letter positions.

**Tradeoff:** Multiple badges, stacked letters, and repeated information increase search effort. Retain only what the new brief needs. Do not copy the blue palette, jazz imagery, or decorative repetition as a template. Inspect whether the date/location can be found without reading every ornament.

## T03 — Hierarchy through grouping and alignment

Source locator: user screenshot 2026-10-05 at 8.58.40 PM.

**Observed:** A narrow publication cover is photographed at an angle in a person's hand. Heavy sans-serif text forms several large groups with stepped left edges and shifts toward the right. Some groups are separated by broad gaps; a small line sits beside a larger group and tiny information appears at the top and bottom. Faint underlying imagery contrasts with the dark text. Much of the major copy has similar apparent weight and scale.

**Interpretation:** Sequence depends on grouping, line breaks, and movement through the page as much as on size. The substantial gaps separate statements even though the typographic voice stays forceful. The hand-held photograph provides context but distorts exact spatial measurements.

**Transfer:** Build related phrases into deliberate blocks; use a limited set of anchors and variable gaps to control sequence. A single family and weight can support expressive hierarchy. Make alignment changes correspond to content structure rather than moving every line arbitrarily.

**Tradeoff:** Similar-size blocks can compete; ambiguous line order may impede reading. The photograph does not establish exact margins, font metrics, paper stock, or an interior system. Do not imitate the wear or texture as a shortcut to credibility.

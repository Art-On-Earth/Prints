# Open Draft typography pilot

## Method and status
Guided SVG concept experiment, 2026-10-05, following the designer's selection of “The unfinished sentence.” No independent baseline. The assistant typeset real SVG text and drew separate vector marks. Browser rendering was inspected. Figma import and native text editability were not tested.

## First result and user feedback
The designer liked the concept and called it progress, but found it too safe. The slash-separated uppercase header felt habitual; the red straight-line corrections felt digital rather than human; hierarchy was confusing; information was repeated; the divider was excessive and misplaced; the red dots lacked a purpose. Font choice was questioned, not conclusively identified as the cause.

## Revision
Removed the header, duplicated date, divider, and dots. Made Open Draft the dominant title with a stepped heavy sans-serif arrangement. Reduced the discarded phrase to a small typewriter-like line, replaced uniform X marks with irregular tapered vector strokes, and added a directional editing gesture. Consolidated workshop information into one lower-left group. Changed the type arrangement without treating serif faces as inherently wrong.

Browser inspection confirmed that the title dominates, mandatory copy is visible, and the added masthead/repeated date are absent. This is the author's inspection, not user acceptance or measured reading performance. The marks are digitally drawn simulations. The second version remains pending designer review; do not record the lesson as proven by this revision.

## Next review
Ask whether the correction feels intentional and whether the title is the first read. Evaluate whether the stronger hierarchy lost too much of the first version's character. Verify no font substitution, clipping, or loss of text editability during Figma import before claiming native delivery.

## Designer review and third revision
The designer preferred the second composition but challenged the supporting hierarchy: event identification, invitation, and logistics had insufficiently distinguishable roles. The third revision makes the event description and date semibold anchors, reduces the invitation's relative size, and groups its lines beneath the description. Time and room remain regular beneath the date. This is a proposed correction, pending visual review and designer acceptance. Preference for the overall second composition does not establish that its information hierarchy passed.

## Third revision — designer feedback
On 2026-10-05, after viewing the differentiated supporting weights, the designer said “better” and explicitly asked to retain the lesson in the skill. Record this as positive qualitative feedback on the revision. The accepted principle is functional hierarchy within secondary copy through weight and grouping. It does not validate Figma import, physical print legibility, or general performance across other briefs.

# Editorial systems

This is AOE's working procedure. The initial AOE visual sample includes a cover, not a complete publication; do not claim the collection proves interior-grid, binding, or reading-performance conclusions.

## Start from content
Map the publication's sections, article lengths, image/caption relationships, opening pages, running information, and index/navigation needs. Use a representative long article and image-heavy spread to develop the system. A cover direction alone is not an editorial system.

## Grid and type
Build columns and margins around the intended reading measure, format, and content—not a universal column count. Distinguish body, headings, captions, footnotes, running heads, and folios using a small reusable set of paragraph and character styles. Treat baseline alignment as a tool: it can organize adjacent columns but should not force uncomfortable leading or crush captions.

Assess hyphenation, rivers, widows/orphans, line endings, and paragraph rhythm in the relevant language. Keep headings with the content they introduce where practical. Fix repeated problems through styles rather than ad hoc per-page adjustments. Preserve reading order when the layout becomes expressive.

## Sequence and physical use
Design turns and transitions, not just isolated spreads. Vary density deliberately; use pacing to support the reading experience. Protect content at folds and the gutter according to the binding and printer's guidance. Obtain the actual stock and binding specifications before calculating a spine. Do not invent page-count divisibility rules without knowing the production method.

Inspect recto/verso placement, folios, contents references, captions, repeated headers, and intentional blank pages. A spread preview does not prove that single-page export will be correct. Follow the printer's required delivery format rather than assuming imposed spreads.

## Proof
Review every page at intended reading size, then review the full sequence as thumbnails. Use actual-size physical samples for fine text and important crops where feasible. Verify missing/overset text and links in the editing tool and inspect the exported file; neither check replaces the other. For a digital edition, reading order and accessible PDF structure require a separate review rather than assuming the print layout handles them.

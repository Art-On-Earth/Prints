# Typography in context — Fonts In Use

Initial research pass: 2026-10-05. Five project pages were read and their displayed artwork inspected in the browser. This is a small starting set, not a survey of the archive. Reported type identifications come from the linked pages; interpretations below are AOE judgments. No artwork or commercial font files are redistributed.

## Selection method
The designer rejected both Bodoni and condensed Roboto Serif in Common Ground. Narrower, heavier, or technically suitable does not imply the right character. Do not treat either rejected trial as an approved AOE default.

Use Fonts In Use as a recurring research source for unresolved typography. Start with comparable communication jobs, then include a contrasting solution. Look at actual compositions, not only isolated font specimens. Record the project, designer, named family, visible treatment, and the reason it could transfer. Separate source attribution from visual inference.

Test actual title words: their repeated letters, counters, terminals, rhythm, cap/lowercase relationship, spacing, and line silhouette. Compare candidates at comparable visual prominence, in the existing composition, before committing. A family category or an adjective such as organic is not an adequate selection rationale. Nature can be communicated through image, material, copy, or composition without requiring a serif. Tall serif is this designer's preference when choosing serifs, not a universal rule.

If a promising face is commercial, link its legitimate source and check availability before using it. Do not silently replace it with an unrelated free font or extract website font assets. Present a clearly labeled alternative when appropriate. Reference gathering is not permission to copy an identity.

## FIU01 — Terrafranta
Source: https://fontsinuse.com/uses/79943/terrafranta-identity-and-packaging
Designer: Céline Hurka. Page reports Tonka as the primary family, with Reverie and Lilian in selected applications; Idyll supplies vegetation-derived illustrative material.
Observed: herb boxes use light uppercase names, distinct colored labels, and fine botanical patterning. The type does not need heavy rustic serifs to coexist with organic material.
Transfer: character can come from restrained letterforms and an integrated material system. Do not copy the color-coded packaging grid into a poster without a reason.

## FIU02 — Environner
Source: https://fontsinuse.com/uses/79896/environner
Page credits a+b studio and lists Cucina.
Observed: pale cards combine lightly weighted serif text with large blue geometric shapes. The small text and bold shapes take different roles.
Transfer: assess type in relation to the visual mass beside it. Do not make both components equally loud just because each is expressive.

## FIU03 — Agnès Varda posters
Source: https://fontsinuse.com/uses/80010/agnes-varda-posters
Designer: Malmsten Hellberg. Page reports a 39-film poster commission, different 205TF families per film, and one family for all text within each poster. Initial group labels Andersen Regular, Helvetius Regular, and Cardone Light.
Observed: narrow posters keep a recognizable arrangement of title, film still, credits, and substantial quiet space while changing typographic voices.
Transfer: consistency need not mean one default font; stable composition can support distinct voices. A single family can carry several information roles. Only initial displayed examples were inspected, not all 39 posters.

## FIU04 — All Things Considered
Source: https://fontsinuse.com/uses/78193/all-things-considered-chelsea-college-of-arts
Designers: Mia Cowie and Tarini Badhadur. Page identifies ALT Picaro, Rafaella with swashes, and Plantin Light Italic.
Observed: the pink thank-you graphic separates compact swashed identity marks from assertive sans-serif copy, with italic connectors providing another reading cue.
Transfer: expressive letterforms can be localized rather than applied to every line. Flourishes must contribute to identity or meaning; this is not a mandate for borders, swashes, or three-family pairings.

## FIU05 — BESIDE, issue 17
Source: https://fontsinuse.com/uses/79921/beside-magazine-numero-17
Art direction/design: Cécile Tousignant. The page lists BC Ludva, Ivar Display, Ivar Text, Neue Montreal, and Area.
Observed: the cover pairs a bold sans masthead with photography; an inspected interior spread combines serif reading columns and a prominent illustration. Exact family-to-element mapping was not independently established.
Transfer: choose display and reading roles separately. A nature-related publication is not confined to one serif mood. Do not infer poster readability from photographed magazine body text.

## Common Ground next decision
Retain the accepted composition and color while evaluating a few genuinely different title voices in context. Explore a distinctive calligraphic or flared form, an expressive tall serif, and a characterful sans as a counterexample. These are research directions, not approved substitutions or claims that the named commercial families are installed. Avoid another unreviewed swap based only on weight and width.

## FIU06 — Eldon Painter, Fantasia
Source: https://fontsinuse.com/uses/79798/eldon-painter-fantasia-album-art
Design/art direction: Kenneth Kegley, Current Studio. Page identifies WS Craft for the sleeve title, Jacquarda Bastarda 9 elsewhere, and Helvetica or similar for notes. Browser inspection of the sleeve shows extremely tall black letters, narrow white counters, tight intervals, and a title spanning the image grid beneath it. The source does not establish that the type was distorted. Transfer the attention to proportion and counter rhythm, not the distinctive letterforms or full layout.
